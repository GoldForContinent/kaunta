Why Kaunta

Built for a bar's real life

Weak Wi-Fi, no change for a phone, debt scribbled on a grit-stained notebook. Kaunta was designed around all three.

Works without internet

Every tap is saved on the phone first. When a connection appears, Kaunta syncs automatically — nothing lost, nothing double-counted.

Cash, M-Pesa & deni

Track every sale by payment type. Debt automatically builds per customer, and the owner can watch it grow in real time.

Live from anywhere

The owner opens owner.html and sees today's take, the heat of the evening, stock vs. sold and the debt ledger — while at home or at the market.

Features

Everything a counter needs

No training needed. If you can tap a bottle, you can run Kaunta.

Split portions

Full / half / quarter pour, priced per size. Stock is tracked in millilitres so the box counts against the shelf — no more straight-up theft.

Full history

Every bottle and shilling, time-stamped, filterable by cash, M-Pesa or debt. Copy a day's report in one tap for WhatsApp.

Stock warnings

Climbing debt, oversold bottles and too many small cash entries all trigger amber alerts instead of surprises at cash-up.

Multiple phones, one bar

Staff join with a 6-digit code from the owner. Every device stays in sync over the cloud — you decide how many on shift.

Debt ledger

Names, amounts and a running total of who is trusted. Clear a debtor in one tap when they settle.

Offline-first & safe

Sales live on the phone until the cloud can take them. Your books are never hostage to a network drop.

FAQ

Before you ask

What exactly is Kaunta?+
A counter app for small bars. The cashier records every drink sold (cash, M-Pesa or on credit) with a tap; the owner watches totals, stock and debt live from anywhere. It works on the phones you already own.
Does it really work without internet?+
Yes. Every sale is saved on the phone first. The moment the phone has *any* connection, Kaunta pushes the outbox to the cloud and pulls everything new down. Nothing is lost and the unique op-ID system means a repeated sync never double-counts a bottle.
How do payments work?+
After the free 30-day trial the bar pays KES 500/month with M-Pesa. In automatic mode the owner taps "Send M-Pesa prompt" and enters the PIN. Without any setup, manual mode shows the Till number + account — the owner pays and verifies the M-Pesa code in the app.
How do I install it on the phone?+
Open this site (https://kaunta.pages.dev) in the phone's browser, tap Install the app → kaunta.html. Android Chrome shows "Add to Home screen" or an install prompt; on iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens full-screen and works offline.
Can more than one phone use the same bar?+
Yes. The owner shares the 6-digit join code from Settings. A staff phone with the code joins the same bar and stays in sync with the owner and every other device.
What happens if the subscription lapses?+
You get a 7-day grace window, then the app locks new sales until payment is confirmed. Your data stays safe in the cloud — the moment you renew, everything syncs again exactly as it was.
Is my money data safe?+
Accounts use email + password (password hashed with PBKDF2), sessions are bearer tokens stored hashed on the server, and everything travels over HTTPS. The database is Cloudflare D1 — a real, redundant cloud database.
